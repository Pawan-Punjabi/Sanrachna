# Sanrachna 🏠

**Transform your room into your dream space.**

Sanrachna is an intelligent, AI-driven platform designed to eliminate the manual burden of interior design. By leveraging Computer Vision and automated spatial analysis, the platform allows users to upload 2D floor plans and receive instant furniture detection and curated product recommendations tailored to their exact space.

---

## 🚀 The Problem
Traditionally, the interior design process is bogged down by manual inefficiencies:
* **Manual Spatial Analysis:** Manually interpreting 2D floor plans is time-consuming and prone to error.
* **The "Visualization Gap":** Difficulty in bridging the gap between a flat architectural drawing and a functional, furnished room.
* **Tedious Sourcing:** Finding furniture that fits specific dimensions and styles requires exhaustive manual research.
* **Scaling Challenges:** Manually identifying layout needs for large-scale or multi-room floor plans lacks the efficiency required for modern workflows.

---

## 💡 The Solution
Sanrachna automates these processes, acting as an intelligent bridge between architectural layouts and functional living.
* **AI-Powered Detection:** Automatically identify layout features and furniture needs from uploaded floor plans using Computer Vision.
* **Smart Recommendations:** Get curated product suggestions from top stores that are matched to the exact requirements of your space.
* **Streamlined Workflow:** Upload a JPG or PNG, or paste a direct image link to receive an instant analysis, eliminating hours of manual tracking.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React.js
* **Backend:** Python Flask
* **Core Logic:** Computer Vision & Data Analytics

---

## 📖 How It Works
As shown in the web interface (`image_bd3e35.png`):
1. **Upload:** Use the "Quick Analyze" dashboard to upload your floor plan or paste a direct image link (e.g., Google Images).
2. **Process:** The system utilizes custom vision algorithms to detect architectural boundaries and furniture layouts.
3. **Visualize:** Browse tailored product recommendations or use the **3D Generator** to transform the flat layout into an immersive visualization.
